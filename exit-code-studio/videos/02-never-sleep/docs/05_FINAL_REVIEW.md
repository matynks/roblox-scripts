# Video #2 — Final independent review of Story Lock v2 / Story Bible v2

**2026-11-15 · requested by the producer before G2 · every correction below is applied in Story Bible v3 / Story Lock v3**

**Method.** v2 was re-read line by line from the files, not from memory. The three statistics were re-verified against primary sources (abstract, RAND release, IEA). Pacing was measured by script from the draft beats.

**Severity scale**

| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Critical** | A factual error that would be narrated, or a logic break that stops the story working |
| **High** | Misleading framing, or a major weakness in the hook or the climax |
| **Medium** | A weakness viewers would notice: clarity, redundancy, transitions, motivation |
| **Low** | Polish |

Finding IDs carry the prefix **FR-** so they cannot be confused with claim IDs in the ledger.

**Totals:** 27 findings — 1 Critical, 3 High, 12 Medium, 11 Low. 25 are corrected in v3; 2 are deliberately left for the script stage (FR-E9, FR-E10).

## 2 · The three statistics

| ID | Sev. | Evidence | Correction (v3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-B1 | **Critical** | **Lighting "roughly 15 %" is outdated.** The IEA's latest figure is about **8 %** of global electricity demand in 2024 (≈ 2,200 TWh, buildings plus outdoor lighting, excluding industry and agriculture). Including industry, estimates reach about 9–12 %. The 15 % came from UNEP's en.lighten programme, before the switch to LEDs. | "Lighting in homes, offices and streets already takes **about eight percent** of the world's electricity." Source S62 (IEA, level A), scope recorded; S46 withdrawn. Class: RESEARCH-SUPPORTED EVIDENCE (estimate). |
| FR-B2 | **High** | **The cold-risk effect was misnamed.** The abstract reports **odds ratios** — 4.24 for 5–6 h (95 % CI 1.08–16.71) and 4.50 for under 5 h (95 % CI 1.08–18.69), compared with over 7 h — in 164 healthy adults aged 18–55, sleep measured for 7 days, then a cold virus given. v2 said "four times **likelier**", i.e. a risk ratio. Further limits: one study; an association; very wide uncertainty. | "In one study, people sleeping six hours or less had **about four times the odds** of catching a cold." On-screen tag: *1 study · 164 adults*. Limits recorded in ledger C03. |
| FR-B3 | Medium | **$411 bn — construct drift.** RAND (2016) estimates the cost of **insufficient sleep** among workers: productivity losses (absenteeism, presenteeism) plus higher mortality, modelled as an upper bound ("up to"; 2.28 % of GDP). v2 said "**Tiredness alone** is estimated…" — the wrong construct, plus an emphasis word the source doesn't support. | "…the cost of **too little sleep**, estimated at **up to** four hundred and eleven billion dollars a year in the US, disappears." Components and limits recorded in ledger G01. |

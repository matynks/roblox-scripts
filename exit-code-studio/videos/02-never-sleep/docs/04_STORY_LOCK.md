## Decisions inside this lock

1. **Cast:** Video #1's Leo, Mia and Mr. Black, plus a new character, Mum. *(Recommended.)*
2. **Age variants:** Leo at 25 and Mia at 18, designed at G5.
3. **The ending:** Leo refuses, the notebook changes his mind, and the "When I have time" chain makes it his own decision *(v3)*.
4. **Optional beats**, kept only if the script's timing allows: short sleepers (ch. 2), dream-simulator adverts (ch. 7), the 29,000-hours line (ch. 8).

## Unresolved risks

1. **One narrator voices the dialogue.** This is Video #1's convention, so Mia's lines will be read by a deep male voice in the climax. *Option at stage 7:* a second ElevenLabs voice for Mia. It's your choice, and an extra generation on your account.
2. **The caption must read at phone size.** "WHEN LEO HAS TIME" has to be legible in about 2 s at 960×540 — check at G5/G6.
3. **The hook's first frame depends on execution.** The eye-reflection pull-back has to be proven in the style samples (G5) and the preview (G6).
4. **The reality line straight after the emotional peak** may still feel like a lecture. Judge it at the preview; the fallback is to shorten it to "Back in the real world, you still need your sleep."
5. **Runtime depends on the wordless beats:** about 7:12 of speech plus about 28 s of designed silence. If the voice runs fast, the film ends near 7:20 — still within the brief.
6. **Some evidence was read only through abstracts and releases:** the RAND model's internals, the shift-work reviews, and the scope of the IEA figure (8–12 % depending on definition). Full texts are blocked by this environment's network policy.
7. **Still open from earlier gates:** new character designs (G5), music and SFX quality (your listening gate, G6a), and the voice choice (stage 7).

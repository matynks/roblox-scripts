1. **One narrator voices all dialogue,** as in Video #1, so Mia's lines will be read by a deep male voice. An option at stage 7: a second ElevenLabs voice for Mia — your choice, and an extra generation on your account.
2. **The page must read in seconds.** WHEN LEO HAS TIME is seen three times and must be legible at phone size within about 2 s each time. The tally must read as "many times" without a countable number. Check at G5/G6.
3. **The "12 May" date must be legible** in chapter 7. If it isn't, the 03:02 message could be read as the same night as chapter 8.
4. **Frame 1 depends on execution.** A crowded playground plus the 3:00 clock must read instantly at phone size, with the thumbnail cut at 0:02 — prove it in the style samples (G5).
5. **The reality line still follows the emotional peak.** Judge it at the preview. Fallback: "Back in the real world, you still need your sleep."
6. **Runtime 7:50 sits near the top of "about 7–8 minutes".** Chapter 4 (+13 %) and chapter 8 (+12 %) are close to the ±15 % tolerance, so the script stage has little room to grow.
